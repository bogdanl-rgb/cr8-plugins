# Message rules

These are my rules for the one message a person writes after a yes on a card.

1. One message, written or edited by a person. It goes from that person's own account.
2. Name the public signal. State the fact and stop. No reason after it, no pitch.
3. One question, last. Nothing after it.
4. Never mention the ad, the click, the site visit or an ad alert. They aren't public.
5. Under about 120 words. A DM in lowercase if that's how you write DMs. An email in sentence case.
6. A different message for each person at the company. Same signal, a different question for each role.
7. Any reply stops the rest. Any no parks the whole company.
8. You wrote last and got silence: park that thread. Wait for a new, dated signal.
9. Check the consent rules where the person lives before you write.

A sample DM:

> hey {first name}, saw you started as VP Marketing at {company} in September. how are you planning the first 90 days?

It states one public fact, then asks one question.
