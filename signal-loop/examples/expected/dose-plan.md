# List dose plan

Inputs you gave: 2,200 people in the ad set's target audience, $35 a day, $80 per 1,000 views (your own price, never a benchmark).

## Monthly spend that holds about 5 views a person a month

2,200 x 40% to 50% reachable in a month x 5 views / 1,000 x $80 = $352 to $440 a month.
Your budget: $35 a day x 30.4 = $1,064 a month. That is over the dose: the list will be used up within weeks. Lower the budget, or move the rest of the budget to the C list or a second signal list from the same market.

## Most live ads this ad set can feed

$35 a day x 14 / $80 = 6.1, so 6 live ads at most (rounded down).
Run 6 or fewer ads in this ad set.
Why: each live ad then gets about 1,000 views in 14 days, my default for when an ad's numbers can be read. Use what the ad set really spends, not the budget field.

The dose caps what one list can absorb. If your lists together hold less than you need, add a second signal list or let the C list carry the rest. Never buy more views for the same people.
The 40% to 50% reachable share, the 5 views and the 1,000 views an ad are my plan and defaults, not measured. Read the real numbers at day 14.
